Security and operation:
- Rabbit Core creates an encrypted mining wallet locally.
- The password and private key never leave the user's computer.
- Back up the keystore and remember the password.
- Never share a private key, password or seed phrase.
- Rabbit Core runs a local full node and validates the chain independently.
- RandomX is used only to earn one persistent admission seat.
- A wallet with an active seat stops admission mining automatically.
- Every active wallet has exactly one consensus seat, independent of CPU speed.
- A second wallet requires its own valid RandomX admission proof.
- Recovery admission reopens after a two-minute chain halt.
- Official bootnodes provide peer discovery only.
- Bootnodes and the public RPC have no consensus or administrative authority.
- Community bootnodes may be supplied by the user.
- Testnet tokens do not represent mainnet tokens or guaranteed monetary value.
