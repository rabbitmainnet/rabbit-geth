Work V2:
- Blocks 1-127 bootstrap a fresh canonical network.
- First admission opens at block 128.
- First canonical admissions activate at block 256.
- At the 10-second target these heights are about 21 and 43 minutes.
- Block height, not a PC timer, controls activation.
- ADMISSION_PENDING means wait without duplicate mining.
- ACTIVE_SEAT means RandomX is complete; keep the node online for LCQ.
- Each wallet has at most one persistent equal seat regardless of CPU speed.
- Rewards use the canonical 70% producer / 30% committee split.
- After a two-minute total chain halt, permissionless recovery opens.
- Recovery resumes from the last canonical block without erasing state.

Safety:
- Back up the encrypted keystore and password separately.
- Never share wallet secrets.
- Stop with Ctrl+C once and wait for clean shutdown.
- Restart with the same wallet and data directory.
- Verify peers and synchronization before diagnosing admission.
- Compare local, public RPC, and explorer heights.
- Bootnodes, RPC, and explorer have no consensus authority.
- Testnet tokens have no guaranteed monetary value.
